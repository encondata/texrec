# Tex Rec — image brief

Deliver as JPG or PNG, no text or logos baked in. Save with the exact file name shown;
they replace the low-res crops in `api/src/seed-images/`.

**Style for every shot (prepend to each prompt):**
Photorealistic scuba photography, natural available light with sun rays, deep blue-teal
water, dark navy shadows, crisp detail, no text, no watermark, no logos. Divers wear black
wetsuits and modern gear; neutral/dark gear colors (no bright branded gear).
